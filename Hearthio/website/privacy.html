1. 适用范围与运营者

本隐私政策适用于家务志（英文品牌名：Hearthio）移动 App 及本官网。家务志是一款本地优先的家庭物品档案与保养记录工具，由 <开发者或运营主体名称> 开发并运营。

家务志不要求注册账号，也不会要求你提供姓名、手机号或邮箱来使用核心功能。当前版本不提供云端账户、跨设备同步、广告或订阅服务。

本政策按当前 1.0.0 版本的项目功能与依赖编写。若正式发布版本新增账号、云同步、分析、崩溃上报、推送或其他联网服务，应在启用前更新本政策和相应授权说明。

3. 信息的使用方式

家务志仅为向你提供已选择的功能而在设备上处理信息，包括：

  • 建立和展示家庭物品、空间、保养计划与生命周期记录；
  • 根据周期、计划日期和本机日历计算到期状态与下一次日期；
  • 按步骤完成保养，并记录费用、耗材、备注和可选照片；
  • 在你授权后安排本地通知，并在设备重启或 App 更新后恢复必要的提醒；
  • 根据真实计划和完成记录生成家庭保养报告；
  • 按你的操作生成 CSV 或 ZIP 备份，以及从你选择的备份文件恢复档案；
  • 维护本机档案完整性，防止无效备份或中断写入覆盖现有数据。

当前版本不将这些信息用于广告、跨 App 追踪、用户画像、数据出售、信用评估或训练第三方人工智能模型。

4. 系统权限

  • 相机：仅在你选择拍照时申请，用于拍摄物品、说明书、保修卡、凭证或保养前后照片。拒绝后仍可使用不需要照片的核心功能。
  • 照片：仅在你选择从照片库添加图片时申请，用于读取你主动选择的图片并复制到 App 私有目录。家务志不会遍历或持续读取整个照片库。
  • 通知：仅在你选择开启保养提醒时申请，用于按各计划的提前天数安排本机通知。拒绝或关闭通知不会阻止创建档案、计划或完成记录。
  • 文件选择与分享：当你恢复备份、导出或分享时，由系统文件选择器或分享面板让你选择来源和目标。家务志只处理你明确选择的文件。

你可以随时在设备系统设置中撤回相机、照片或通知权限。撤回后，对应功能可能不可用，但已保存在 App 中的档案不会因此自动删除。

5. 第三方服务、共享与披露

当前版本没有自建账号服务器，不接入广告网络、行为分析 SDK 或跨 App 追踪服务，也不出售个人信息。

  • 设备操作系统服务：相机、照片选择、本地通知、文件选择与系统分享由 iOS、Android 或相应系统组件提供，其处理受平台条款与隐私政策约束。
  • 你选择的分享目标：当你通过系统分享面板将 CSV、ZIP 或其他文件发送给某个 App、联系人或云盘时，该接收方会按其自身政策处理文件。
  • 隐私政策网页：App 仅在你打开“隐私政策”时加载本官网的 HTTPS 页面。网站托管商可能处理标准网络日志，详见第 8 节。

若法律要求、有效法律程序或保护用户与公众安全确有必要，我们可能依法披露实际掌握的有限信息。但由于核心档案默认只在你的设备上，我们通常无法查看或提供这些本机内容。

6. 数据保留与删除

  • 物品、计划、记录、设置和照片会保留在 App 私有存储中，直到你在 App 内修改或删除、从备份替换档案，或按操作系统方式移除 App 数据。
  • 删除物品或保养记录时，不再被其他记录引用的相关 App 内照片会一并从当前设备清理。
  • CSV、ZIP 或分享后的文件保留在你选择的位置，由你和相应接收方管理。删除 App 内数据不会自动删除这些外部副本。
  • 本地通知可在 App 或系统设置中取消或关闭；操作系统可能按其机制保留必要的通知调度信息。

设备备份、文件云盘或其他系统级服务是否保留副本取决于你的设备和服务设置，不由家务志控制。

7. 你的控制与权利

你可以直接在 App 内查看、修改和删除物品、计划与记录，导出 CSV，创建或恢复完整备份，并在系统设置中管理相机、照片和通知权限。首次进入时创建的示例净水器按普通物品使用，无需在设置中单独管理。

由于当前版本没有云端账号或开发者数据库，运营者通常无法远程访问、更正或删除你设备上的核心档案。若你对政策、权限、数据路径或无法自行完成的操作有疑问，可通过第 12 节的邮箱联系我们。你所在地区的适用法律可能赋予你其他权利。

8. 官网的数据处理

9. 安全、存储地点与跨境处理

家务志采用本地优先、系统沙盒、按需权限、临时文件限制、备份格式校验和原子写入等措施降低风险。但任何电子存储方式都无法保证绝对安全，请保护设备锁屏凭据，并谨慎选择导出与备份文件的保存位置。

核心档案默认位于你的设备。你主动分享文件后，数据可能由你选择的 App、联系人、云盘或其基础设施在其他国家或地区处理。官网标准日志的处理地点取决于 <网站托管服务商> 的基础设施。

10. 儿童隐私

3. How information is used

Hearthio handles information on your device only to provide the features you choose, including:

  • creating and displaying home items, spaces, maintenance plans, and lifecycle records;
  • calculating due states and next dates from intervals, planned dates, and the local calendar;
  • guiding maintenance steps and recording costs, materials, notes, and optional photos;
  • scheduling local notifications after permission is granted, including restoring necessary reminders after a device restart or app update;
  • creating home-maintenance reports from actual plans and completion records;
  • creating CSV or ZIP backups at your request and restoring an archive from a file you select; and
  • protecting archive integrity so an invalid backup or interrupted write does not replace existing data.

The current version does not use this information for advertising, cross-app tracking, profiling, data sales, credit scoring, or training third-party AI models.

4. System permissions

  • Camera: requested only when you choose to take a photo of an item, manual, warranty card, receipt, or the state before or after maintenance. Core features that do not need photos remain available if you decline.
  • Photos: requested only when you choose to add an image from your library. Hearthio reads the image you select and copies it into the app-private directory; it does not continuously browse your full library.
  • Notifications: requested only when you choose to enable maintenance reminders. Declining or disabling notifications does not block items, plans, or completion records.
  • File selection and sharing: the system file picker or share sheet lets you choose a source or destination when restoring, exporting, or sharing. Hearthio handles only the files you select.

You can revoke Camera, Photos, or Notifications access at any time in system settings. The related feature may become unavailable, but revoking a permission does not automatically delete records already stored in the app.

5. Third-party services, sharing, and disclosure

The current version has no first-party account server, advertising network, behavioral analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking service. Hearthio does not sell personal information.

  • Operating-system services: camera access, photo selection, local notifications, file selection, and system sharing are provided by iOS, Android, or the relevant platform component and are governed by the platform's terms and privacy policy.
  • Share destinations you choose: if you send a CSV, ZIP, or another file to an app, person, or cloud drive through the system share sheet, that recipient handles the file under its own policy.
  • Privacy-policy webpage: the app loads this website over HTTPS only after you open Privacy Policy. The site host may handle standard network logs as described in Section 8.

We may disclose limited information actually in our possession when required by law, valid legal process, or a genuine need to protect users or the public. Because core records stay on your device by default, the operator generally cannot view or provide that on-device content.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Items, plans, records, settings, and photos remain in app-private storage until you change or delete them in the app, replace the archive from a backup, or remove app data through the operating system.
  • When an item or maintenance record is deleted, related in-app photos that are no longer referenced by another record are also removed from the current device.
  • CSV, ZIP, and shared files remain wherever you chose to send or save them. Deleting in-app data does not automatically delete those external copies.
  • Local notifications can be cancelled or disabled in the app or system settings. The operating system may retain the scheduling information needed under its own mechanisms.

Whether device backups, cloud drives, or other system-level services retain a copy depends on your device and service settings and is not controlled by Hearthio.

7. Your controls and rights

You can view, edit, and delete items, plans, and records in the app; export CSV; create or restore a full backup; and manage Camera, Photos, and Notifications access in system settings. The sample water purifier created on first launch behaves like a regular item and needs no separate management in Settings.

Because the current version has no cloud account or developer database, the operator generally cannot remotely access, correct, or delete core records on your device. Contact us using Section 12 if you have questions about this policy, permissions, data paths, or an action you cannot complete yourself. Applicable law in your region may give you additional rights.
